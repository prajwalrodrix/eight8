Performance Auditing Framework

D

Daily Operational Reviews

Team leaders review the previous day's KPI performance against SLA targets. Deviations trigger a Root Cause Analysis template before the morning stand-up.

W

Weekly Performance Reports

Structured client-facing report covering volume, quality scores, SLA attainment, and agent performance distribution. Shared by 09:00 Monday.

M

Monthly Governance Calls

Attended by senior leadership on both sides. Covers trend analysis, upcoming changes, compliance reviews, and the next month's improvement priorities.

Q

Quarterly Business Reviews

Executive-level review of the engagement's strategic alignment, commercial performance, and 90-day forward plan. Presented on-site or via video conference.
