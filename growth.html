The Client Journey

From Onboarding to Continuous Improvement

Every Growth & Transformation engagement follows a structured progression. You will always know exactly which phase you are in and what comes next.

Phase 1 — Discovery & Audit (Weeks 1–2)

Current-state assessment of your CX ecosystem, technology stack, lead flows, and conversion data. We map where value leaks before proposing a single change.

Phase 2 — Strategy Design (Weeks 3–5)

Delivery of a prioritised transformation roadmap with business case modelling, resource requirements, risk assessment, and a 90-day quick-win plan.

Phase 3 — Implementation (Months 2–6)

Staged rollout of agreed initiatives — technology integrations, process redesign, agent enablement, and lead generation program deployment — with weekly progress reporting.

Phase 4 — Optimisation & Scale (Ongoing)

Monthly performance reviews, A/B testing cycles, and incremental improvements against agreed KPIs. The engagement deepens as results accumulate.

Continuous Improvement Loop

Quarterly business reviews with executive sponsorship, benchmarking against industry peers, and annual strategy refresh to ensure your CX stays ahead of the market.
