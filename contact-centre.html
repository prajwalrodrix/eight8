Technical Assistance (Tier 1–3)

Dedicated technical support desks staffed with certified agents trained to your product specifications. Escalation paths and knowledge bases are built before go-live — not improvised during incidents.

Tier 1 — First-Line Resolution

Password resets, account queries, basic troubleshooting — resolved at first contact using structured playbooks.

Tier 2 — Advanced Support

Configuration support, product defect triage, and integration troubleshooting with warm transfer to Tier 3 when required.

Tier 3 — Specialist Escalation

Managed escalation to your engineering or product team with full incident documentation and SLA tracking.
